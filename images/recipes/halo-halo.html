Halo-Halo

A refreshing and colorful Filipino dessert!

About This Recipe

Halo-Halo is a popular Filipino dessert made with shaved ice, milk, sweetened fruits, beans, jelly, and delicious toppings. It is perfect for hot days!

Recipe Information

Halo-Halo Preparation Details
Preparation Time 20 minutes
Cooking Time No cooking required
Servings 2 glasses

Ingredients

  • 1 cup shaved ice
  • 1/4 cup sweetened banana
  • 1/4 cup sweetened jackfruit
  • 1/4 cup cooked sweetened beans
  • 1/4 cup nata de coco
  • 1/2 cup evaporated milk
  • 1 scoop ube ice cream
  • 1 slice leche flan

Instructions

  1. Prepare a tall serving glass.
  2. Add the sweetened fruits, beans, and nata de coco.
  3. Fill the glass with shaved ice.
  4. Pour evaporated milk over the ice.
  5. Add ube ice cream on top.
  6. Finish with a slice of leche flan.
  7. Serve immediately and enjoy!
