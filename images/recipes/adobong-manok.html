Adobong Manok

A classic Filipino chicken dish full of delicious flavors!

About This Recipe

Adobong Manok is one of the most popular Filipino dishes. It is made by cooking chicken with soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, peppercorns, and bay leaves. It is delicious when served with warm steamed rice.

Recipe Information

Adobong Manok Preparation Details
Preparation Time 15 minutes
Cooking Time 40 minutes
Servings 4 people

Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram chicken, cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional)
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Wash and prepare the chicken.
  2. Combine chicken, soy sauce, and crushed garlic in a bowl.
  3. Let the chicken marinate for at least 15 minutes.
  4. Heat cooking oil in a pot.
  5. Brown the chicken pieces lightly.
  6. Pour in the marinade, vinegar, and water.
  7. Add bay leaves and peppercorns.
  8. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat.
  9. Cover and simmer until the chicken is fully cooked and tender.
  10. Add sugar if desired and adjust the seasoning.
  11. Serve hot with steamed rice.
