Kare-Kare

A traditional Filipino stew with rich and creamy peanut sauce!

About This Recipe

Kare-Kare is a traditional Filipino dish known for its thick peanut sauce and delicious vegetables. It is commonly prepared with beef or oxtail and served with bagoong and steamed rice.

Recipe Information

Kare-Kare Preparation Details
Preparation Time 20 minutes
Cooking Time 2 to 3 hours
Servings 4 to 6 people

Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram beef or oxtail
  • 8 cups water
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup toasted rice flour
  • 1 teaspoon annatto powder
  • 2 eggplants, sliced
  • 1 bunch string beans
  • 1 bunch pechay
  • 1 banana blossom, sliced (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Bagoong for serving

Instructions

  1. Wash and prepare the beef or oxtail.
  2. Place the meat and water in a large pot.
  3. Add the onion and bring to a boil.
  4. Lower the heat and simmer until the meat becomes tender.
  5. Remove the meat and reserve the broth.
  6. Mix the peanut butter with some warm broth until smooth.
  7. Add the peanut butter mixture and annatto powder to the broth.
  8. Add the toasted rice flour and stir until the sauce thickens.
  9. Add the eggplant and string beans.
  10. Cook until the vegetables are tender.
  11. Add the pechay and cook for another 2 minutes.
  12. Return the meat to the pot.
  13. Season with salt and pepper.
  14. Serve hot with steamed rice and bagoong.
